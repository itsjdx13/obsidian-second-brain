# Small Team Service Fit

Headcount, delivery deadlines and payment flexibility can matter more than a headline meal price.

## Evidence from the comparison report

[[01 Projects/RocoBroker/Catering/Knowledge Base/03 Resources/Comparisons/Catering Options Final Report]], pages 1–3, visually reviewed 2026-09-16:

- Narijeh: organizational service described as inactive below 20 people.
- Rouhi Kerman: report marks unsuitable because of a 15-portion minimum and no Friday delivery.
- Arghavan: report associates bulk orders with 200 portions in the initial notes; confirm actual applicability.
- Snappfood organizational: prior-night ordering and consolidated next-day delivery.

## New takeaway evidence — captured 2026-09-16

[[01 Projects/RocoBroker/Catering/Knowledge Base/03 Resources/Suppliers/Narijeh/Sources/Narijeh Takeaway Message - Captured 2026-09-16]] clarifies that Narijeh allows weekly takeaway orders through cashiers, with cash settlement below 10 portions. This is a separate service scope: do not treat the older below-20 organizational threshold as a general refusal to serve small teams.

[[01 Projects/RocoBroker/Catering/Knowledge Base/03 Resources/Suppliers/Snappfood/Sources/Snappfood Activation Message - Captured 2026-09-16]] reports the company panel active, but does not resolve minimum quantity, restaurant coverage or delivery terms.



## Interpretation

Treat minimum quantity and arrival time as hard eligibility gates before scoring price or variety. A low price cannot compensate for a service that cannot serve the actual team.

No headcount or required delivery time has been supplied. These report claims are not current supplier confirmations.
