# Price Normalization

Compare the same service scope, not just the displayed meal price.

## Proposed calculation
For a common order size:

`landed cost per person = (meal subtotal + tax + delivery + packaging + other mandatory charges - applicable discounts) / people served`

Include charges only once: an all-inclusive quote must not have its included components added again.

## Required fields
- Original amount and explicitly stated currency; do not assume rial or toman.
- Unit: meal, person, day, package, or another basis.
- Order quantity and minimum-order rule.
- Inclusions/exclusions and quote-validity date.
- Source page and date of any subsequent confirmation.

If conversion is necessary, record the conversion rule and source; preserve the original value. A Mehr menu requires its year/calendar to be established before treating it as current.
