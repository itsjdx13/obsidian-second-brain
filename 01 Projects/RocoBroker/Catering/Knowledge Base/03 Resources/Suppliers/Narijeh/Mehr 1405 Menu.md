# Mehr 1405 Menu

Source: [[01 Projects/RocoBroker/Catering/Knowledge Base/03 Resources/Suppliers/Narijeh/Sources/Mehr Menu]], page 1; text extracted and visually checked 2026-09-16.

## Confirmed scope

The printed dates run from 1405/07/01 to 1405/07/30 in the Solar Hijri calendar. This is a dated schedule, not proof of availability at order time.

The table has five meal categories: rice-based, non-rice dishes, diet-labelled salads, sandwiches and a fifth option, plus accompaniments for the first two categories. Ordinary Saturday–Wednesday rows generally offer all five; Thursday and Friday rows are narrower.

## Representative entries

| Date, Solar Hijri | Rice-based | Non-rice / second column |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1405/07/01 | Chicken kebab | Stuffed pepper/eggplant |
| 1405/07/07 | Gheymeh eggplant stew with rice | Herb kuku |
| 1405/07/20 | Bean rice with beef pieces | Vegetable lasagna |
| 1405/07/30 | Celery stew with rice | Juicy burger sandwich |

Friday rows repeat chicken kebab and koobideh. The table's category placement differs on reduced-choice days; consult the original for the full row.

## Interpretation

Menu variety is evidenced, but a diet-labelled salad is not proof of vegetarian, vegan, calorie-controlled or allergen-safe suitability. Obtain ingredient and cross-contact details rather than inferring them from dish names.
