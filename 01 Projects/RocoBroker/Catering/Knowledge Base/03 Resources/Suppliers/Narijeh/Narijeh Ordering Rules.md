## Document rules

1. Register orders in the supplier panel two weeks in advance.
2. Finalize 48 hours beforehand with a 3% difference allowance.
3. Same-day changes are allowed until 09:00 with a 2% tolerance.
4. Additions after that time incur a new delivery charge.
5. Pay before the week for the stated 25,000 toman/portion discount, or settle the weekly invoice within two working days.

The source does not fully define tolerance calculation, rounding, cancellations, or absence treatment. Confirm these in writing.

## Interpretation

A changing headcount may conflict with this planning model. Do not assume the discount or settlement terms apply to retail orders below the report's 20-person threshold.
