### Price and schedule limits

CMA published fees are examination-plus-PDF amounts, not classroom tuition or an all-inclusive team budget. The regulator says to follow the e-service fee and may change amounts. Risk in Financial Services is another possible route: exam + PDF AED 1,320, resit AED 962 (E-005); only shortlist it if the job mapping calls for it. Do not convert currencies or multiply by assumed headcount.

EIMF page lists an expired 4 September discount and a syllabus ending 30 September despite its October intake. Its schedule changes from GMT+3 to GMT+2 on the final session; confirm UAE times. The advertised 15 hours differs from the sum of listed session windows. Cyprus subsidies must not be assumed applicable to this team.

### Official delivery vs third party

Official CMA exam booking exists; CISI also supports self-study and training partners. Existing E-004 demonstrates historical regulator workshops, but no current CMA MLRO/Compliance classroom intake was confirmed. A CMA–CISI relationship does not prove that every private preparation course is CMA-approved. Ask separately about examination recognition, provider/course approval and CPD eligibility. Mauritius licensing or training portability is not established.
