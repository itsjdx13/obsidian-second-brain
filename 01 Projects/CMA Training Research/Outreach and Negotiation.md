## پیش‌نویس استعلام از CMA
Subject: MLRO and Compliance training — official provision and recognition

Dear [CMA department/contact],

We are researching MLRO and Compliance training for [participant profile and intended purpose] in [jurisdiction]. Could you please clarify:

1. Does CMA directly provide these courses, or are they offered by third parties or through joint arrangements?
2. Where can we find current course dates, delivery locations/modes, fees and entry requirements?
3. Is there an official list of recognized providers or courses, and how can we verify a specific course?
4. For [provider, exact course title/code and certificate], is it accepted for [specific intended purpose]? Please indicate the recognition reference, scope, validity and conditions.
5. Are any additional examinations, experience requirements or approvals necessary for that purpose?

Please share official references or direct us to the relevant department.

Kind regards,
[Name / organization / contact]

## پیش‌نویس استعلام از شرکت آموزشی
Subject: MLRO / Compliance course — schedule, total fees and CMA recognition

Dear [Provider],

Please send the current brochure and a written quotation for [course] for [number] participant(s), including:

- Exact course title/code, organizer and certificate issuer.
- Location, delivery mode, language, next dates, time zone, duration, registration deadline and availability.
- Entry requirements, documents, syllabus, attendance, assessment, pass requirements and certificate details.
- Currency and itemized total fees, including tax, exams, certificates, materials and resits; quote validity and payment terms.
- Cancellation, refund and rescheduling terms.
- Any CMA recognition: official reference or document, exact scope, validity and accepted professional purpose.

Please distinguish provider licensing from recognition of this particular course or certificate.

Kind regards,
[Name / organization / contact]

## مذاکره پس از استعلام
- درخواست تخفیف گروهی متناسب با تعداد واقعی و امکان برگزاری اختصاصی.
- بررسی اینکه آزمون، گواهی، منابع و آزمون مجدد در مبلغ پیشنهاد لحاظ شده‌اند یا نه.
- بررسی زمان‌بندی مناسب، مدت اعتبار پیشنهاد و شرایط پرداخت/انصراف.
- ثبت پیشنهاد اولیه، درخواست ما، پاسخ نهایی و شروط کتبی؛ سقف بودجه و اختیار تعهد هنوز مشخص نشده‌اند.
- هیچ وعدهٔ ثبت‌نام، پرداخت یا تعداد قطعی بدون تصمیم کاربر ثبت نشود.
