# Catering Trial

## Outcome

Test whether a candidate can serve the actual team reliably before making a continuing commitment. This is a proposed workflow, not an approved purchase.

## Before ordering

- [ ] Confirm decision owner, headcount, location, budget and dietary needs.
- [ ] Obtain written price unit, validity, inclusions and minimum order.
- [ ] Confirm arrival window and complaint/refund process.
- [ ] Agree the trial size; the report proposes 10–20 portions for Fajri.
- [ ] Confirm whether Narijeh offers its reported free sample.
