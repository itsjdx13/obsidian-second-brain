# Evidence and Provenance

## Claim contract
A reusable factual claim should contain:
- The claim, with units and scope.
- A link to its source note and original document.
- Page/section locator.
- Printed document date or “unknown.”
- Review date and any uncertainty.

## Separate three layers
**Source fact:** what the document explicitly says.
**Interpretation:** what you infer and why.
**Decision:** what you choose under stated constraints.

Supplier promotional claims are attributed statements, not independent verification. Filenames establish inventory, not prices or document authority.

## Change handling
When a new quote contradicts an old one, retain both sources, explain scope/date differences, and update dependent knowledge and decisions. Do not erase the earlier rationale.
