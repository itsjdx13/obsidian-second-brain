# Evidence, Interpretation and Decisions

Generalized from [[projects/RocoBroker/Catering/Methods/Evidence and Provenance]]. The supplier-specific note and original sources remain in place.

## Source evidence
Record what the source says, its date, scope, units and page or section. Link the original and state uncertainty. A supplier claim is an attributed statement until independently checked. Preserve conflicting sources with their dates.

## Interpretation
Explain what you infer, the evidence used, assumptions and unresolved questions. A proposed ranking or AI recommendation is an interpretation, not an approved choice.

## Approved decision
Record the actual choice, decision owner, approval date and evidence of approval. Link supporting evidence and interpretation; include constraints, rationale and a review trigger. Leave approval fields blank until confirmed. Preserve previous decisions when conditions change.

Use [[notes/Templates/Source Template]] and [[notes/Templates/Decision Template]].
